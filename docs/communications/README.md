# Communications Guide

Congratulations on taking the first step towards organizing your community event or Club chapter! We are always excited to see Hack Clubbers running their own projects, and the magic of community run events. These guidelines are written for events / hackathons / projects and Clubs that are run by members of the Hack Club community but are not directly backed by Hack Club HQ. 

## What language can I use in emails / phone calls to potential donors and sponsors?

We ask that you make it clear that you are not a part of Hack Club HQ. You are more than welcome to mention that you are a member of the Hack Club community, a Hack Club “chapter” (for clubs), or fiscally sponsored by the Hack Foundation (if you’re running on HCB)\! 

* To that end: Please include in your email this line: *This event is run by teenage members of Hack Club, and is not a Hack Club staff sponsored event.* 

Here are some examples of a good email copy:

* Hey\! My name is Orpheus and I’m one of the organizers of Orpheus Hacks. We are fiscally sponsored by Hack Club, a 501(c)3 nonprofit.  All donations made to us are tax deductible.  
* Hey\! My name is Orpheus and I’m one of the co-leads of Orpheus Hacks, a local chapter of Hack Club.  
* Hey\! My name is Orpheus, I’m a junior in high school, and a member of the Hack Club community.  
* You can mention past Hack Club events later in the email, that’s fine.   
* *Just be sure to add a line that says: **This event is run by teenage members of Hack Club, and is not a Hack Club staff sponsored event.*** 

## What logos can I use on my website?

You are more than welcome to use any Hack Club logo as long as it’s in the sponsors / supporters section of the website. For chapter events like Scrapyard and Counterspell, please feel free to add the Hack Club 2025  banner on your website. For more information about our visual brand identity, please visit [hackclub.com/brand](http://hackclub.com/brand). 

If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to me at [dev@hackclub.com](mailto:dev@hackclub.com).
